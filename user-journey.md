Alice wants to meet other fans who enjoy exchanging sketches, but her short social media bio says little about her interests. She has tried making a separate introduction page and found arranging the text and pictures tedious. She registers an account on Fandom Friends and logs in to create an introduction she can use to find people with more specific interests in common.

In the bio creator, Alice adds fandom tags for *Frieren* and *Haikyu!!*, a pairing tag for *Fern × Stark*, and interaction tags for **Sketch swaps** and **Episode discussions**. As she types, autocomplete suggests existing tags. Alternate names lead to the same tag: typing “Delicious in Dungeon,” for example, suggests *Dungeon Meshi*, so fans using either name can find one another. Selected tags appear as labeled chips grouped under Fandoms, Characters and Pairings, and Interactions. Each chip has a star control to mark a current favorite and a remove control to delete it from the bio.

The app arranges Alice's selections into a bio card and shows a live preview beside the editor. Alice stars *Frieren* and *Fern × Stark*, then uploads one of her sketches for the starred pairing. The preview refreshes to give these favorites more space and place the sketch beside the pairing. Her other interests remain visible as smaller tags. She can see how her introduction will look without positioning each element herself.

Alice saves the card as “Frieren and sketch swaps,” selects it as her displayed bio, and enables **Open to New Friends**. Other users can now discover that card and search for its tags. Only her displayed bio is public; additional saved bios remain private until she chooses to display one. She can turn off Open to New Friends whenever she wants to pause discovery.

Alice opens the discovery page, where she can browse the displayed bios of other users who are open to new friends. She types “Frieren” into the tag filter and selects the suggested tag. Search uses the same tag suggestions and alternate names as the creator. Results must contain every selected tag; selecting **Sketch swaps** as well would narrow the results to people with both interests. Search filters are separate from her own bio, so she can explore an interest without adding it to her public introduction. Each result shows a bio card with the tags matching her filters highlighted and starred favorites visibly marked.

Among the Frieren results, Alice finds Bob, whose bio also features *Fern × Stark* and **Sketch swaps**. She clicks **Interested**, and the button changes to **Interested ✓**, privately recording her interest so she can return to his profile. She also reads Charlie's bio, which lists *Frieren*, *Dungeon Meshi*, and **Episode discussions**. Charlie is mainly looking for discussion partners, while Alice currently wants someone to exchange sketches with. She clicks **Pass**. His card stays in her results with muted styling and a **Passed** label, while remaining readable and clickable. This status is visible only to Alice. Opening the card again preserves her choice; she can select **Undo Pass** to clear it or **Interested** to replace it.

A few weeks later, Alice becomes absorbed in *Dungeon Meshi* and wants people to discuss it with. She reopens her saved bio in the creator, adds *Dungeon Meshi* and *Laios* tags, and stars them along with **Episode discussions**. She unstars *Frieren*, keeping it as a secondary interest, and removes *Haikyu!!* and *Fern × Stark*. The preview updates to reflect these choices and removes the sketch associated with the deleted pairing. Alice clicks **Save as New Bio**, names the card “Dungeon Meshi discussions,” and chooses **Display This Bio**. Her earlier card remains saved, while the new card and its tags become her public introduction.

Alice searches for *Dungeon Meshi* and **Episode discussions**. Charlie's bio appears in the results because it contains both selected tags and he is still open to new friends. His card retains its muted styling and **Passed** label. Alice clicks it to read his introduction again and realizes that his interest in episode discussions now fits what she wants. She selects **Interested**, replacing her earlier pass and returning the card to its normal appearance.
